# Product Context: Onyx

## Why Onyx Exists?

Modern students, particularly Gen Z, struggle with consistency and focus in an increasingly distracting digital world. Existing habit trackers often feel too corporate or simplistic. Onyx aims to bridge this gap with an aesthetic that resonates with Gen Z while providing powerful productivity tools.

## Problems It Solves

- **Lack of Motivation:** Uses streaks and social "flexing" to keep users engaged.
- **Distraction:** Integrated Pomodoro timer (Focus Mode) helps maintain deep work sessions.
- **Inconsistent Progress:** Visual progress bars provide immediate feedback on daily goals.

## User Experience Goals

- **Striking Visuals:** "Dark Mode / Neon" aesthetic to stand out.
- **Ease of Use:** Simple habit logging and focus timer activation.
- **Portability:** Access habits anywhere, with future cloud sync.
- **Customizability:** Widget stores and theme selections to make the app feel personal.

## Key Workflows

- **Onboarding:** Visually striking entry point with multiple login options.
- **Habit Management:** Adding (limited for free users) and tracking habits.
- **Focusing:** Setting a timer and working without ads (for pro users).
- **Sharing:** Creating social media ready images of streaks.
