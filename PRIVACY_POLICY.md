# Privacy Policy for Onyx: Habit Tracker & Focus

**Last updated:** September 4, 2026

Welcome to **Onyx: Habit Tracker & Focus** ("Onyx", "we", "our", or "us"). We are committed to protecting your privacy and personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how your information is collected, used, and safeguarded when you use our mobile application across Apple App Store and Google Play Store, in compliance with GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation), KVKK (Personal Data Protection Law of Turkey), CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act), and Apple/Google store policies.

---

## 1. Information We Collect

### A. Information You Provide
- **Account Information:** When you register via Google Sign-In or Email, we collect your email address, display name, and unique user identifier (UID) provided by Google Firebase Authentication.
- **Habit and Productivity Data:** Habits, streaks, completed dates, break habits, and Pomodoro focus session minutes are stored locally on your device and optionally synchronized with Google Cloud Firestore when signed in.
- **Profile Customization:** Profile picture (if chosen using device camera/photo gallery) is stored locally or in your Firebase profile.

### B. Automatically Collected Information & Third-Party SDKs
- **Google AdMob:** We display non-personalized and personalized banner, interstitial, and rewarded ads. AdMob may process advertising identifiers (IDFA on iOS, GAID on Android), IP address, and general diagnostic data. Users in the European Economic Area (EEA) and the UK are presented with the Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent form to manage their ad choices.
- **RevenueCat:** For processing in-app purchases and subscriptions, RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and transaction receipts to verify Pro status. We do not store or process credit card numbers; all transactions are securely processed by Apple App Store and Google Play Store.
- **Google Firebase:** Used for authentication and cloud database synchronization.

---

## 2. How We Use Your Information
- To operate, maintain, and provide the core habit-tracking and focus timer features of Onyx.
- To synchronize your streaks and habit data across your devices.
- To verify Pro subscriptions and restore purchases.
- To display advertisements complying with age ratings and regional advertising standards.

---

## 3. Data Protection, GDPR & KVKK Rights
Under GDPR (EU) and KVKK (Turkey), you have the right to:
- **Access and Portability:** Request a copy of your personal data stored on our servers.
- **Rectification:** Edit your profile information at any time within the app settings.
- **Erasure ("Right to be Forgotten" / Account Deletion):** You can delete your account and all associated habit data at any time via **Settings -> Delete Account** in the app. Upon deletion, your records in Firebase Authentication and Cloud Firestore are permanently erased.

---

## 4. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)
Onyx is intended for general audiences aged 13 and older (or the applicable age of consent in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.
